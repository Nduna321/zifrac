About Muneni Retail Partners Africa

Muneni Retail Partners Africa was incorporated in 2014, with our head office in HARARE and an experienced staff compliment of 30 with agencies countrywide. We retail electricals, gas, solar, phones & accessories, bedding and related products with a focus on quality, customization and customer satisfaction.

  • Supplier of high-quality products with a history of customer satisfaction and value for money.
  • Custom solutions and branding options for businesses and promotions.
  • Reliable logistics and accessible customer support.

Our Vision

To be recognizable worldwide for creating long lasting connections through inspiring product design and excellent value addition.

We aim for continuous improvement, customer satisfaction, and global expansion while fostering sustainable relationships across industries.

Our Mission

Provide innovative products and make a positive community impact.

We are committed to delivering products that meet our customers' needs while upholding the highest standards of customer service and teamwork, and through corporate social responsibility initiatives.

Our Values

Reliability, Quality Assurance, Respect, Consistency, Trustworthiness, Passion, Accessibility.

We are dependable and authentic, pursuing defect-free quality standards while treating customers and staff with dignity and respect.

Why Choose Muneni Retail

No minimum orders, proven product quality and competitive pricing.

We offer customization, attention to detail, logistics support, and products available with or without your company logo — making Muneni Retail the practical choice for your home and business solutions.
