End-to-end retail sourcing and logistics delivery

ZIFRAC sources quality electricals, solar, gas, phones, and bedding while providing reliable logistics and in-store support across Zimbabwe. Our team works with partners to design practical inventory flows, optimise last-mile delivery, and ensure products reach customers on time.

  • Fast procurement and supplier coordination tailored to your SKU mix.
  • Secure warehousing and efficient logistics for consistent stock availability.
  • Dedicated account managers and 24/7 customer support for retail partners.

We focus on measurable retail outcomes: reduced stockouts, improved delivery predictability, and higher customer satisfaction through reliable product assortment and service.

After onboarding, ZIFRAC trains your store teams, provides merchandising support, and continuously optimises inventory and delivery processes so your retail operations scale smoothly.
