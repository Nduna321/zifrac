Smart City Command Center Deployment

ZIFRAC implemented a single operational picture for Harare's transport and retail operations. We integrated logistics workflows, normalised inventory telemetry, and delivered responsive dashboards for both back-office and field staff.

The platform now surfaces critical alerts in under two seconds, helping coordinators respond faster to outages and incidents. Automated reports feed city leadership every morning, while AI-assisted workflows recommend the next best action for each department.
